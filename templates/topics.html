{% translate "Speeches are turned into word weights (TF-IDF: words frequent in a speech but rare overall weigh more), then split into topics with non-negative matrix factorization (NMF). A topic is a set of words that tend to appear together; the name shown is its strongest words unless an editor has given it a title. Procedural phrases and party names are left out so that topics group speeches by subject. Topics are found by the method, not chosen in advance, and one speech can touch several; only its main topic is counted here." %}
